Principles

Autonomy with accountability

The question is no longer whether AI agents will run business processes — it is whether you can prove, years later, why each action was allowed.

Bounded actors

Agents operate with explicit, capability-scoped authority. They cannot exceed the scope, duration or risk level they were delegated.

Propose, never decide

Document extraction, anomaly flags and recommendations are proposals. The governed execution layer — not the model — holds authority.

Human approval where it matters

You declare in the workflow definition which steps require human sign-off, from whom and under what conditions.

Decision context is evidence

Each action records the historical context, inputs, policy version and capability it relied on — deterministically reconstructable.

Revocation without downtime

If an agent or an external partner's agent loses authorization, its grants can be revoked without stopping healthy processes.

Prompt injection & jailbreaking

A manipulated model should still be powerless

No filter makes a language model impossible to trick. Nitrotick takes a different approach: the model never holds authority, so a jailbroken or injected agent cannot act beyond what it was granted.

Authority lives outside the model

Credentials, policies and grants stay in the governed execution layer. Injected instructions can change what an agent proposes — not what it is allowed to do.

Every action checked at execution time

Each proposal is verified against the agent's capability, scope and current policy version before any state changes. Out-of-scope actions are blocked, not merely logged.

Attempts become evidence

Blocked and held actions are recorded with their inputs and context, giving security teams and auditors a reconstructable trail for NIS2 and DORA incident handling.

Can Nitrotick stop prompt injection and jailbreaking?

Nitrotick does not try to make a language model impossible to manipulate. It removes authority from the model: every action an agent proposes is checked against its delegated capability and policy before it executes, critical steps wait for human approval, and every attempt is recorded as evidence. A manipulated agent can still produce a bad proposal, but it cannot execute beyond its scope.

How does Nitrotick support the EU AI Act, NIS2 and DORA for AI agents?

Nitrotick provides technical evidence: tamper-evident records of every agent action, human oversight gates, deterministic reconstruction of inputs and policy versions, and atomic revocation of authority. It supports your compliance programme and does not replace risk assessments, documentation or conformity procedures.

Does the AI model get direct access to our systems?

No. The model proposes; the governed execution layer holds the credentials and authority. Agents receive capability-scoped grants limited in scope, duration and risk level, which can be revoked at any time.

What is A2A governance?

A2A (agent-to-agent) governance controls how AI agents hand work to other agents: which agent may delegate what, under whose authority, and with what evidence. Nitrotick enforces it at execution time — every A2A hand-off is checked against capability and policy and recorded as tamper-evident evidence.

What is the difference between local and federated A2A?

Local A2A runs inside one organization, on its own infrastructure and under its own policies. Federated A2A connects agents of independent organizations through Nitrotick Federation: each organization keeps its own node, data and authority, and only signals, claims and signed evidence cross the boundary.

Is Nitrotick an AI gateway?

No. An AI gateway controls access to AI models and routes requests, and an A2A governance layer defines how agents interact. Nitrotick Execution OS provides the full execution environment around them: identity, capability authorization, durable state, governance and cryptographic evidence for the whole workflow.
