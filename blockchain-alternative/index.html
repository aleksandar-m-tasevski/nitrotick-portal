Stablecoins & settlement

Do you need a stablecoin — or a verified process?

Stablecoins solved simple token transfer. But moving money on-chain does not synchronize business state: personal data, business logic and authority still live in fragmented off-chain systems.

Nitrotick does not compete with money. It verifies, secures and governs the business process before settlement — and then hands the closed state transition to whichever rail you use.

  1. Enterprise stackAI agents, regulatory processes and personal data stay inside your domain.
  2. Nitrotick engineValidates every state transition deterministically, in under 100 ms, with zero gas.
  3. Settlement rail of your choiceRegulated stablecoins, CBDCs, SWIFT or SEPA move the capital — only after the state is proven.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Nitrotick a private blockchain?

No. Nitrotick has no ledger replicated between nodes, no consensus protocol, no tokens and no smart contracts. It provides provenance, integrity and independent verification through deterministic execution, tamper-evident evidence and cryptographic commitments.

How is integrity protected without consensus?

Every step, approval and state transition is committed to append-only, tamper-evident evidence with cryptographic commitments. Any later change is detectable, and the history can be deterministically reconstructed and independently verified.

Can a third party verify a Nitrotick proof?

Yes. Proofs can be verified independently without access to your internal systems or raw data. For regulated and legally sensitive use cases, Nitrotick Qualified Anchor adds an external qualified trust anchor.

Can Nitrotick work together with stablecoins or CBDCs?

Yes. Nitrotick verifies and closes the business state transition first; settlement then runs over the rail you choose — regulated stablecoins, CBDCs or traditional banking rails such as SWIFT and SEPA.

Is Nitrotick compatible with GDPR?

Personal data stays in your systems of record and remains erasable there. Nitrotick federates signals, claims and proofs that contain no personal data, avoiding the conflict between immutable public ledgers and the right to erasure.

Can Nitrotick work together with an existing blockchain?

Yes. With Nitrotick Verifiable Off-Chain Execution & Oracle, complex and confidential workflows run off-chain under multi-party governance, and only a cryptographic commitment to the proven result is anchored on your blockchain or DLT network. Each network is connected through a dedicated adapter built for its finality and event model.
