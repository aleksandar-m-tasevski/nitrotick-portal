Regulatory context

The EU is making product data verifiable

Under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) and sector rules such as the EU Battery Regulation, Digital Product Passports become a market-access requirement — product group by product group.

Manufacturers, importers and suppliers must be able to show what was recorded about a product, where it came from and which version is valid — across supply chains, over the product's whole lifecycle.

Every Nitrotick asset carries

  • Versioned digital state
  • Lineage and traceability
  • Durable execution evidence
  • Cryptographic proof
  • Independent verification
  • Audit-ready history

Why not a blockchain passport?

Proof — without the public-ledger baggage

Public-ledger passport

  • Business and personal data written to immutable public networks collides with GDPR
  • Gas fees, token management and smart-contract audits
  • Data leaves your systems and your control
  • Specialized, expensive engineering talent

Nitrotick verifiable asset

  • Data stays in your systems; proofs are verifiable independently
  • Zero gas, predictable infrastructure cost
  • Integrates with your ERP, GS1 identifiers and registries
  • SaaS, private cloud or on-premise
