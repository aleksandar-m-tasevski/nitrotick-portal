Why an execution OS

AI can decide. Machines can act. Who governs the execution?

AI agents now plan work, robots carry it out and the outcome reaches suppliers, carriers, utilities and regulators. Each of them runs its own software, under its own authority.

Between the decision and the physical action there is usually nothing that checks who is allowed to issue the command, keeps the process alive through failures, or proves afterwards what really happened. That missing layer is what Nitrotick provides.

  1. Which agent or person issued this command?
  2. Did it hold the authority at the moment of execution?
  3. Does the process survive a crash without repeating a physical action?
  4. Can a partner organization join without handing over its data?
  5. Can an auditor verify the outcome years later?

FAQ

Execution OS — frequently asked questions

What is a Federation-Enabled Execution OS?

It is the execution layer that sits between decision-makers — AI agents, people and enterprise systems — and the machines and processes that act on those decisions. Nitrotick checks identity and authority, executes each step deterministically and durably, federates work across independent organizations and records verifiable evidence of every state transition.

Is Nitrotick an AI gateway?

No. An AI gateway controls access to AI models and routes requests, and an A2A governance layer defines how agents interact. Nitrotick Execution OS provides the full execution environment around them: identity, capability authorization, durable state, governance and cryptographic evidence for the whole workflow.

Does Nitrotick replace a robot operating system or a PLC?

No. Robot controllers, PLCs and real-time control loops stay where they are. Nitrotick governs the missions, commands and hand-offs above them: who may issue a command, under which authority, and with what evidence of the outcome.

How is the Execution OS related to Nitrotick Federation?

Federation is one of the core services of the Execution OS. It lets independent organizations execute shared processes while each one keeps its own node, data and authority. The same OS also powers IP Evidence & Verification, Digital Product Passports and governed AI execution.

Where does Nitrotick run?

In your cloud on Kubernetes, in a private cloud or on-premise. Storage stays in your infrastructure, and Nitrotick connects to industrial systems such as MES, SCADA and IoT platforms as well as existing enterprise systems.
