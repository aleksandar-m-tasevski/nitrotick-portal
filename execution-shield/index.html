When authorized access is not enough

A valid login does not mean an operation should execute

Authentication confirms identity. Permissions define access. But critical business operations often need something more: independent authority, governed execution and verifiable accountability.

Traditional security controls who can access your systems. Nitrotick governs which critical operations may actually execute.

FAQ

Execution Shield — frequently asked questions

How does Execution Shield work between institutions?

Nitrotick enables independently governed operations across institutional boundaries. Each participating organization retains control over its own authorization policies, execution decisions and evidence. No shared central authority is required for institutional execution decisions.

Is Nitrotick Execution Shield a firewall or cybersecurity product?

No. It does not replace your identity provider, firewall, endpoint security, SIEM or API gateway. Those controls decide who can access your systems. Execution Shield governs whether a selected critical business operation is authorized to execute.

What happens if an attacker steals valid credentials?

A stolen identity is still limited to the scope it was granted. For operations configured and validated to require independent approval, one compromised identity is not enough to execute them — provided the independent approvers are not compromised as well.

Do we need to replace our ERP or core banking system?

No. Execution Shield protects selected operations of existing systems through controlled integration. The protection applies to operations that pass through the enforced execution boundary, so any direct path to the same operation must be closed as part of the integration.

Does Execution Shield prevent every attack?

No. Its value is limiting what a compromised or unauthorized participant can do to critical business state, and retaining verifiable evidence of protected execution decisions. Guarantees depend on the integrated operations, the deployment configuration and completed acceptance testing.

Is it only for organizations that work with partners?

No. A single organization can protect its own critical operations. When operations cross into partners, the same model extends to independently governed organizations through Nitrotick Federation.
