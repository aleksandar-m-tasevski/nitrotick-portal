The trust & evidence problem

Can you prove it — five years from now?

Can you independently prove why a transaction was allowed, which identity authorized it, which policy governed it, what inputs produced the outcome — and that the historical state has not been altered?

If the answer is “no”, you have a trust and evidence problem. Logs tell you what happened. Regulators, auditors, partners and courts increasingly ask why it was allowed and how the state was reached.

Not just what happened — but why it was allowed, how the state was reached, and whether the outcome can still be independently verified years later.
  1. Who was authorized?
  2. Which policy governed the execution?
  3. Which exact definition was applied?
  4. What inputs generated the outcome?
  5. How was the target state reached?
  6. Can an independent party reconstruct the exact sequence years later?
  7. Can organizations trust each other without surrendering data sovereignty?

How it works

Secure multi-tenant federated execution

Raw web traffic between organizations carries no native trust. Nitrotick moves state transitions and cryptographic metadata over its own federation protocol — each hand-off verified, every result signed.

Agent-to-agent delegation

AI agents act as bounded, governed actors across institutional boundaries. They propose; execution authority and cryptographic hand-offs remain locked in the governance layer.

Dynamic ancestry & atomic revocation

Capabilities are re-verified across the federation hierarchy at execution time. A partner that loses authorization is revoked atomically — healthy peers keep running.

Proofs without PII

Every state transition commits signed evidence to an immutable audit trail, without exposing underlying personal or proprietary enterprise data.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Nitrotick a blockchain?

No. Nitrotick delivers the core business utility people expect from blockchain — tamper-evident history, provenance, integrity and independent verification — through deterministic execution, hash-chained evidence and cryptographic commitments. There are no tokens, no gas fees, no public ledger and no smart contracts to audit.

Do we need to replace our ERP or core systems?

No. Nitrotick operates above your existing systems of record such as ERP, MES, PLM, core banking or insurance platforms. They keep their role; Nitrotick adds the execution, governance, evidence and federation layer across them.

How does Nitrotick handle GDPR?

Personal data stays in your own systems. Nitrotick federates signals, claims and cryptographic proofs rather than raw records, so evidence can be verified independently without exposing or permanently publishing personal data.

What is the difference between Nitrotick Proof and Nitrotick Qualified Anchor?

Nitrotick Proof provides independent cryptographic proof, immutable evidence and deterministic reconstruction for critical execution. Nitrotick Qualified Anchor adds an external qualified trust anchor to those proofs for regulated and legally sensitive use cases.

How is Nitrotick Federation different from workflow orchestrators or APIs?

Workflow orchestrators are built for internal microservices, and plain APIs carry no native cryptographic trust. Nitrotick Federation is built for execution across independent organizations: each keeps its own node, data and authority, capabilities are re-verified at execution time, revocation is atomic and every hand-off produces signed evidence.

Which deployment models are available?

Nitrotick is available as SaaS, in a private cloud or on-premise, with custom integrations into your existing systems, registries and workflows.
